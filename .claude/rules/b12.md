## Model 2 snapshot_069 (observed 2026-09-04)

IC ID blocks, decoded from per-type min/max — every boundary exact:

    LMC  gas 1-300,000            dm 300,001-400,000      disk 400,001-1,400,000
    SMC  gas 1,400,001-1,700,000  dm 1,700,001-1,714,000  disk 1,714,001-1,814,000

- Blocks sum exactly to `N_LMC_INIT`/`N_SMC_INIT`. SMC dm is **14,000** (B12 Table 1
  misprint). Max ID 1,814,000 → **no live MW particles**; MW is an analytic potential.
  `galaxy_mask` validated (0 unassigned of 468,486 stars). A perturber-direction
  `reference` cannot come from this snapshot.
- **Gas carries the high flag bit too.** Never restrict the parent-ID decode to stars.
- **Star IDs are not unique** and collide with gas IDs. Fine for range-based
  `galaxy_mask`; **never track a star across snapshots by ID.** Flag bit is orthogonal
  to formation time.
- All star particles share one mass, 1831.48 Msun.
- **315,969 of 335,102 LMC stars have formation time exactly 0** (~95% of new-star
  mass, one bin) — almost certainly a clock reset from a relaxation run. **Exclude from
  any SFH or mean SFR**; an `age < 0.1` cut is unaffected. Asked Rathore, unanswered.
- Present day: 2,664 LMC stars < 100 Myr, Sigma_SFR integrating to **0.0488 Msun/yr**
  (observed LMC ~0.2). 1-D `sfh()` first four bins average 0.0486. **Regression pin** —
  checks the `h` factors, `dt * 1e9`, `pixel_area`, and the transpose at once.
- SFH is flat ~0.036 Msun/yr once ft==0 is removed. No decline, no pericenter burst in
  this window — don't hunt for physics in an artifact of that bin.
- **Decided 2026-11-11:** `b12.GADGET_TIME_TO_GYR` → 0.9778 (clouds_demo's 0.97 was
  ~0.8% short). Products record it in `meta` (`b12.conventions()`). The 0.0488 pin was
  measured with 0.97 → re-measure after the change; don't "fix" the code to hit 0.0488.
- **B12 figure x-axis is lookback time** from each model's present-day snapshot (user,
  2026-11-11). SFH bins end exactly at `t_now`.

Every B12 driver currently repeats readsnap → to_kpc → galaxy_mask → age inline
(`lmcsmcmw/scripts/sfr_maps_b12.py`) — the motivation for a per-source loader.

## Rathore's `clouds_demo` (read and reproduced 2026-09-04)

Unit conversions, parent-ID decode and density centre are reproduced in
`datasets/b12.py` and `analysis/frames.py`. Its `phi = -arctan(abs(Ly/Lx))` is correct
only for the LMC's Q4 axis; `arctan2(Ly, Lx)` is the fix.

## Frames

- **`shrinking_sphere_center(tol=0.01)` stops early on tidally extended tracers.** LMC
  disk stars span 48.8 kpc from the COM; thin outer shells move the centre < 10 pc, so
  `patience=2` is met at tens of kpc. `tol=1e-4` *or* `r_init=10` converges to 0.088 kpc
  of Rathore's centre; `shrink` is irrelevant. It returns only the centre — returning
  `(final_radius, n_iter)` would have made this a 5-second diagnosis.
- Face-on rotation: see `.claude/rules/maps.md` (Gram–Schmidt, `reference` default).
